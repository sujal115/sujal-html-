Animal Facts

Fastest Land Animal
Cheetah

Cheetahs can run up to 60 miles per hour in just 3 seconds, making them the fastest runners on land.

Sea Creature
Octopus

An octopus has three separate hearts, blue blood, and can squeeze through tiny cracks to escape danger.

Super Sleeper
Koala

Koalas live in eucalyptus trees and sleep for up to 20 hours every single day to save energy.
