My Top 3 Foods

Pepperoni Pizza

Cheesy, greasy, and always best fresh out of the oven. I could eat this every single day.

Cheeseburger

A juicy beef patty with melted cheddar cheese, lettuce, and extra pickles on a toasted bun.

Chocolate Ice Cream

The perfect dessert. Cool, rich, and even better when you add colorful sprinkles on top.
